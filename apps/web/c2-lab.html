About this engineering prototype

Engineering prototype, not finished anatomy. The CC0 body uses a calibrated skeleton and source skin weights. Shoulder fidelity, skin volume, sever-ready topology and final silhouette acceptance remain under evaluation. This is not the C4 editor.

Drag to rotate, pinch/wheel to zoom. Pose targets are deterministic diagnostic rotations, not authored combat animation. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
